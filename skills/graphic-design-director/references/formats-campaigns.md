# Formats and coordinated campaigns

## Input integrity
- Copy dates, times, venues, prices, contacts and CTA exactly from the brief; do not guess missing information.
- Preserve supplied logo and official imagery; do not redraw a trademark without permission.
- Treat each format as a sibling composition sharing the same system, not an identical crop.

## Common digital starts (verify platform requirements at delivery)
| Format | Example starting canvas | Composition priorities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Square social post | 1080 × 1080 px | One clear hook; medium density; CTA visible at feed scale |
| Portrait feed post | 1080 × 1350 px (4:5) | Strong upper hook; use added height for supporting facts |
| Story/Reel cover | 1080 × 1920 px (9:16) | Protect text from platform UI/safe overlays; avoid tiny footer |
| Landscape slide | 1920 × 1080 px (16:9) | Big claim, high-contrast data or image, few ideas per slide |
| A4 print | 210 × 297 mm | Work in real units; 3 mm bleed common if printer requires it; verify |

Actual social safe zones and export constraints change. Treat listed dimensions as **examples**, not unchanging platform requirements. For professional print, confirm trim, bleed, ICC profile, stock, minimum line widths and CMYK conversion with the print provider.

## Campaign recipe: flyer + square + story + CTA variant
1. Establish unchanging tokens: logo usage, primary palette, font roles, motif, image treatment and brand voice.
2. **Primary flyer:** communicate full event detail, strong concept and supporting text.
3. **Square feed:** elevate one hook; fewer words; easy-to-scan details; CTA.
4. **Story:** recompose vertically, increase type scale and ensure UI-safe positioning. Do not shrink the flyer into a narrow center column.
5. **CTA graphic:** one action-oriented message; optional deadline or offer only when verified.
6. Inspect all four together: same recognizable campaign; different compositions suited to each platform.

## Delivery checklist
- Correct spelling, typography, facts and asset versions.
- Sufficient contrast at output size; readable location/time/CTA.
- All required text visible and not clipped on export.
- Text editable when requested and feasible; fonts available/licensed; links and QR codes tested.
- Export format appropriate to purpose: PNG/JPEG for raster digital, PDF for print, SVG/vector master when appropriate.
- No claim of print readiness without actual bleed/profile checks; no claim of accessibility conformance without checking relevant criteria.

## Reject and repair
- **Reject:** 4:5 flyer simply stretched to 9:16. **Repair:** rebuild the information modules vertically.
- **Reject:** CTA lost behind a mobile app's overlays. **Repair:** shift to safer central area and reproof.
- **Reject:** “final” output with placeholder contact details unmarked. **Repair:** label clearly or request accurate data.
