# Illustration, imaging, composition and visual storytelling

**Conceptual influences:** Scott McCloud, James Gurney, Marcos Mateu-Mestre.

## Before making the image
- Clarify its job: explain, dramatize, symbolize, set mood, create identity or draw attention.
- Choose a level of abstraction suited to the message. Simplify what is nonessential while preserving identifying information.
- Establish composition with thumbnail sketches: focal point, movement direction, depth planes, negative space for type.
- Choose visual language: line weight, contour style, texture level, lens/camera behavior, shape vocabulary, palette and shadow logic.
- Maintain internally consistent lighting direction, material behavior, perspective and depth cues unless inconsistency is a deliberate stylistic device.

## Image direction methods
- **Cinematic:** controlled key light, restrained highlight management, strong value grouping, human story.
- **Graphic vector:** limited shape language, clean silhouette, legible at small sizes.
- **Textured editorial:** restrained grain/paper, flat color masses, distinctive crops; texture should feel material, not generic noise.
- **Collage:** clear logic for photographed vs hand-rendered vs typographic layers, believable overlaps or knowingly surreal ones.
- **Surreal conceptual:** one clear impossible interaction that reinforces the message rather than arbitrary strangeness.
- **Culture-led:** use accurate motifs, clothing, environments and context only where researched or supplied; avoid treating cultures as generic style kits.

## Composition checklist
- Can it read in silhouette? Is the focal point obvious? Is any text over a cluttered region? Do perspective and scale feel intentional? Are hands, faces and objects anatomically/geometrically plausible when realism is required? Are details accurate and respectful?

## Reject and repair
- **Reject** five incompatible materials competing in one hero image. **Repair:** establish one material logic and one deliberate contrast.
- **Reject** fake lettering embedded inside generated imagery. **Repair:** keep important text as editable typesetting where possible.
- **Reject** image crops that remove the main subject or culturally meaningful detail. **Repair:** change focal crop to protect message.
- **Reject** “beautiful” ambient effects that obscure the hierarchy. **Repair:** simplify the scene and reserve quiet space for type.

## “What these books would reject” heuristic
Rendering detail does not rescue a weak silhouette or storytelling composition. Communication comes first.
